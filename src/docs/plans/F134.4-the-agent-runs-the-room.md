# F134.4: The agent runs the room

> **Plan.** The fourth child of [F134](F134-the-showroom-looks-modern.md). The guide's bar is at the foot of the
> showroom since F134.3. This child lets the guide run the room from there: its answer stands as a caption beside the
> place it lights, its choices are drawn in that caption, a demo's play waits while it talks, and the whole talk opens
> as a drawer at the side.

**Status:** built unattended on Louis's word of 2026-11-15: "Finish the whole F134 unattended, stop and show before
merge. want to check locally". The look is the J and K mockups', which he saw with F134's design.
**Branch:** `feature/F134.1-the-start-is-a-stage`, where F134 is being finished.

## Context

F134's design: "When it talks about a place it lights that place, its words stand beside it, and its choices are
drawn there." Its rule 2: nothing moves unless an agent's tool call caused it. Its done-when for this child: a live
walk of the start and of one demo page, by the real guide, moves the stage.

**What was looked up.**

- *What already moves.* The guide's `ShowPage` lights a place through the widget's script (`showArea`): it scrolls to
  it, opens a card, turns a paged section and spotlights it for four seconds. Tried live on the local site: asked
  "Show me the restaurant", the guide showed `/demos/restaurant-demo` and the start brought the restaurant forward,
  since a dock button is a card the script opens. So a window coming forward on `ShowPage` works today.
- *The choices.* An `Offer` call's choices are read by `OfferCall.Of` into `Offered`, a question and its choices,
  each with a label, a reason and whether it is suggested. Today they are drawn in the talk.
- *The demo's play.* `RecordedChat` plays a recording turn by turn through `TurnReplay` and can be played again from
  a turn. It has no way to wait.
- *The mockups' caption* (`F134-mockups/agent.js`): the guide's name, a title, its words, its choices, and "Or ask me
  anything, in your own words"; placed to the lit place's right if there is room, else under or over it.

## Design

**The caption.** `AgentCaption` shows the guide's last answer: its words, and the choices of its last `Offer`, the
suggested one marked, each asking through the widget. A last button puts the visitor in the box. While the last turn
showed a place, the caption takes the folded talk's place over the bar.

**Beside the place.** On a wide screen the caption stands beside the place the guide lit, to its right where there is
room, else under it, placed by the widget's script. With no place, or on a phone, it stands over the bar.

**The play waits.** `RecordedChat` gains `Hold`: while it is on, the play waits between its steps. A business's demo
holds its play while the guide is talking, read from a small shared state the bar keeps, `GuideActivity`.

**The talk as a drawer.** "Show the whole talk" opens it as a drawer at the side of the page on a wide screen, and
as a sheet over the bar on a phone.

## Steps

One commit each, test first. Unattended.

1. **The plan**, its four pictures and its rows. *Test:* none (docs).
2. **The caption.** The last answer's words over the bar while the last turn showed a place, and a button into the box.
   *Test:* the caption shows the last words when a place was shown, and the folded talk otherwise.
3. **The caption's choices.** The last `Offer`'s choices, the suggested one marked, each asking. *Test:* a choice asks
   its label; the suggested is marked.
4. **Beside the place.** The script places the caption beside the lit place on a wide screen. *Test:* the module's
   names are the names the styles read, and a phone gets none.
5. **A window comes forward.** *Test:* the start brings forward the demo whose address the guide showed.
6. **The play waits.** `GuideActivity`, `RecordedChat.Hold`. *Test:* a held play does not move on, and goes on when let
   go; a business's demo holds while the guide talks.
7. **The talk as a drawer.** *Test:* the open talk is a drawer on a wide screen and a sheet on a phone.
8. **Checked live, docs and done.** A walk of the start and of one demo page by the real guide, its cost reported. The
   web's and the site's design docs, the write-up, the work log, this plan deleted.

## Concepts

Skill 4 (tool design): every movement on the page is a tool call's effect, never a script of its own (F134's rule 2).
Skill 20 (extensibility): a player that can wait, told by a state any part can read.

## Approved during the build

- **2026-11-15, unattended.** Louis: "Finish the whole F134 unattended, stop and show before merge".
- **2026-11-15, step 2 as it was built.** `AgentCaption` shows the last answer's words with the guide's name, a
  label for the box (a press puts the visitor in it with no script) and a button to the whole talk. A turn knows
  whether it showed a place and what it said, `Turn.ShowedAPlace` and `Turn.Words` (ADR 0123). The bar shows the
  caption while the last turn, done, showed a place and said something; otherwise the folded talk. The test was
  first written over a streamed turn, which failed in the client's stream reader on a text block after a tool's
  result, a fixture shape problem and not the caption's; it now hands the bar a runner whose chat holds the turn.
- **2026-11-15, step 3 as it was built.** The caption's choices are the talk's own `OfferCards`, so a choice looks,
  marks its suggestion and answers (a box choice too) the same in both places, rather than a second drawing. Whether
  an answer is a caption, and what it holds, is a small record beside the caption, `Captioned.Of(turn)`: the turn
  stays in `Chats`, which reads nothing below it. An answer that showed a place and only offered choices, with no
  words, stands as a caption too. Open for Louis to overrule.
