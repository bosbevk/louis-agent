A cache write is priced at its lifetime's rate per million tokens: the 5-minute writes (`CacheWrite` minus `CacheWrite1h`) at `CacheWrite5m`, and the 1-hour writes at `CacheWrite1h`, with cache writes also counting toward the long-prompt tier threshold. `cache_write_1h` is not a separate token kind but the part of `CacheWrite` that was written with a 1-hour lifetime, and it is null when the provider doesn't report the split, in which case all writes are priced at the 5-minute rate.
