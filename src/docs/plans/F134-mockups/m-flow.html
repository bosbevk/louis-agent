A flow in the new look

Four drawings, each on a dark page and on a light one. They are drawn by hand from two real pictures of the docs, in the colours and icons the tool has, to settle how a flow looks before its renderer is built.

1. Parts and calls: the web's picture

No shapes, since nothing here is a decision or a start. A box stands level with the box that feeds it wherever a row is free, so a line from one box to one box runs straight across: the chat page to ChatShell, the admin layout to AdminShell, ChatRunner to TurnStream, AgentApi to louis-agent.api. A line bends only where one box feeds several or several feed one. At AgentApi the violet and the green line arrive at two points, the upper source at the upper one.

2. A procedure: how the orchestrator uses a runbook

This one is a procedure, so it has shapes: its start is a pill and its two questions are diamonds. It runs down, so level here means in line: each box stands under the box that feeds it where it can. The last box has two arrows in, from the diamond's "no" and from "anything else, or in doubt", and they arrive side by side.

3. When two lines would share a stretch

Lines in a gap turn on one line as long as their upright pieces are clear of each other. Only when two uprights would lie on one another does the second get a track of its own, a step beside the first.

4. The shapes a box may have

What this design settles, if it is approved

  • The title. A flow's title up to its first colon is the picture's title, and the rest is the line under it. No field is added to the JSON.
  • A box. A panel tinted in its tone. At its left a solid bar in its colour, as a component's panel has, then its icon in a solid badge of that colour, then its words in the sans face.
  • A shape that means something. A decision, data and a start or an end have their outline at full strength with a soft glow in their colour, so the eye finds them first.
  • A group. A lifted panel behind its boxes, its label at the top left.
  • Where a box stands. Level with the box that feeds it, where a row is free, so the line between them is straight.
  • Where a line turns. Every line of a gap turns on the same line. A second track, a step beside it, only for an upright piece that would otherwise lie on another.
  • A line. The colour of the box it leaves, a small ring where it leaves, dots that march to its arrowhead.
  • A word. A small pill on its line, in the line's colour.
  • The key. Under the drawing, across its width: a row for each tone and each shape the picture uses.
  • A diamond shows no icon, and its words stand in the middle under a short bar in its colour. Its shape already says what it is.
