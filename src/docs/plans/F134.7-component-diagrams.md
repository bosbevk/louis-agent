# F134.7: Component diagrams

> **Plan.** A child of [F134](F134-the-showroom-looks-modern.md): a new kind of picture in our own diagram tool, a
> component diagram, drawn from JSON as every other kind is, in a new look. Then one in the design doc of every
> project that has components.

**Status:** approved 2026-11-14 (Louis: "go, commit the lean and write the plan and execute"). **Branch:**
`feature/F134.1-the-start-is-a-stage`, where F134.1's parts are (Louis: "so we build this and add it to F134 when done").
**Design:** [l-component-diagram.html](F134-mockups/l-component-diagram.html), opened in a browser from the repo.

## Context

Reading the web's design doc after F134.1's parts were built, Louis asked for pictures of the components
themselves: "we should add component diagrams", then "we should add component diagram as an type". The tool draws
five kinds today (`cards`, `flow`, `sequence`, `classes`, `ticket`), and none shows what a component holds, what is
handed down to it and what it says back up.

His words for how:

- "we will build our own in diagrams", "it must be generated by json as all the others".
- "try a new style fancier than we have", then of the drawn design: "go".
- "all projects which has components should have an component diagram in their documentation".

**What was looked up.**

- *How a component diagram should look.* By the UML a component is a box, what it offers is a ball and what it
  needs is a socket, a box may hold other boxes, and a port is a connection point on its edge. The C4 model's
  component level zooms into one thing and shows the components inside it and what they touch outside, each with a
  name, its kind and a line of what it does. C4 prescribes no notation. Ours takes the nesting, the ports on the edge
  and the name-kind-line from them, and draws them its own way.
- *How a kind is built here.* A kind is a part of `DiagramSource`, records for its JSON, checks in `DiagramReader`,
  a layout that places everything, and an `IDiagramRenderer`. `louis-agent.doc-diagrams` draws every
  `diagrams/*.json` through `DiagramRenderers.RenderTitled`, which puts the docs' title bar, legend and note round
  whatever the renderer drew. So the tool itself needs no change.
- *Which rules reach it.* `DocsDiagramsTests` holds every SVG to its JSON byte for byte, and every picture to a doc
  that shows it. `FileSizeTests` and `FolderSizeTests` hold the new folders. `DiagramReader` builds its messages'
  holes as unnamed objects, which ADR 0119 has changed as a file is touched: step 2 does that before step 3 adds
  to it.
- *Which projects have components.* Eleven hold `.razor` files: `louis-agent.web`, `.web.accounts`, `.web.evals`,
  `louis-agent.site`, the three demo apps (`code-review`, `puls`, `starter`) and four packs' web views (`files`,
  `git`, `recipes`, `triage`).

**What could not be confirmed, and the step that will.** Whether the lines from a box's state to the pins it feeds
can be routed by a rule, for any nesting, without crossing a box. Step 6 proves it. If it does not hold, the pins
the state feeds are drawn lit and joined by no line, and the plan says so under *Approved during the build*.

## Design

**What a component diagram shows.** One component, everything it holds, and what stands around it.

- **A box inside a box is held by it.** The boxes are stacked top to bottom in the order the JSON names them.
- **A pin on a box's top edge is what crosses it.** A filled pin with an arrow down is a parameter handed down. A
  hollow pin with an arrow up is a callback said back up.
- **A box may hold one thing of its own**, such as which demo is forward. It is drawn as a pill under the box's
  name, with lines down to the pins it feeds and lines back up from the callbacks that change it.
- **What stands around the root** is drawn beside it: before it, what hands things to it or what it reads, and
  after it, what it calls, such as the script module beside a component.
- **A ghost** is a box drawn dashed: what stands in the same place when the usual one does not.

**The JSON.** A `components` part with a `root` box and a list `around`. A box has an `id`, a `name`, a `kind` (the
few words in its chip), a `note`, a `tone`, its pins as two lists of names (`in`, `out`), the boxes it `holds`, and
a `state` with what it `says` and the pins it feeds `down` and hears `up`, each named `box.Pin`. A neighbour has an
`id`, a `name`, a `side` (`before` or `after`), the box it stands by (`to`, the root when not given), what it
`gives`, whether it only `reads`, and the words on its lines (`calls`, `back`).

**A tone means what it means in every picture** ([Draw a diagram](../guides/draw-a-diagram.md)): green is this
project's own part, violet the framework's, blue who starts it, teal state and data. The design was drawn in amber
and blue before that rule was read. The real kind takes each box's `tone`, and the docs' legend names the ones
used.

**The new look.** Everything the renderer draws inside the docs' frame:

- Panels get lighter the deeper they nest, each lifted by a soft shadow, with a marker in its tone.
- Names in a sans face, code words in the monospace one.
- Lines carry small dots that march the way the data flows, and stand still for a reader who asked for less
  motion.
- The ground is a faint grid of dots. No gradient mixes two colours.

The docs' frame stays the shared one (title bar, legend, note), so a component diagram sits among a doc's other
pictures as one of them.

**The layout places everything.** No position is written in a JSON. A box is as wide as its name, its pins, its
note and the boxes it holds need, and the boxes it holds stretch to its inner width. A neighbour stands in a column
beside the root, level with the box it talks to.

## Steps

One commit each, test first.

1. **The plan**, its four pictures, the design in `F134-mockups/`, and its rows in the TODO, the work log and the
   plans' index. *Test:* none (docs).
2. **The reader's holes are records.** Each unnamed object `DiagramReader` hands a message becomes a named record,
   declared and then passed. *Test:* each record's properties are its message's holes. The reader's tests stay
   green.
3. **The JSON and its checks.** The kind, the records, and what is refused: no root, an id used twice, a state
   that names a pin or a box that is not there, a neighbour beside a box that is not there. *Test:* each refusal,
   by its message, and a whole picture read back.
4. **The layout.** Sizes bottom up, places top down, pins on top edges, neighbours beside their box. *Test:* every
   held box lies inside its holder, no two boxes of one holder overlap, a pin stands on its box's edge, and a
   neighbour is level with the box it talks to.
5. **The renderer and the look.** The SVG, light and dark, inside the docs' frame, with its legend. *Test:* a
   golden SVG of the showroom's start, and its words inside the drawing. Looked at in a browser, both themes.
6. **The state's lines.** A lane for each line from a pill to a pin and back. *Test:* no line crosses a box it does
   not join. If no rule holds for any nesting, the fed pins are drawn lit and this step says so.
7. **The guide and the skill.** *Draw a diagram* gains the kind with a whole example and its fields, and the skill
   names it. *Test:* none (docs), the guide's example is one the reader reads.
8. **The framework's web.** A component diagram in the design docs of `louis-agent.web`, `.web.accounts` and
   `.web.evals`, each read from the code. *Test:* the docs' picture tests.
9. **The site and the demos.** The same for `louis-agent.site`, `code-review`, `puls` and `starter`.
10. **The packs, the rule and done.** The same for the four packs' web views. `DocsDiagramsTests` gains the rule:
    a project that holds components shows a component diagram in its doc. The diagrams' design doc, the write-up,
    the work log, F134's plan gains this child, and this plan is deleted. *Test:* the new rule fails before the
    last picture and passes after.

## Verification

- Each step runs `louis-agent.diagrams.tests` and `louis-agent.doc-diagrams.tests`, and `louis-agent.core.tests`
  when it adds a folder or a file.
- Each picture is looked at as an image, light and dark, before its step is committed.
- No paid run.

## Concepts

- Step 3: a schema that refuses a wrong picture with what to change, as a tool's input is checked. Skill 18

- Steps 4 to 6: a layout as a pure function of data, tested with no browser. Skill 18.
- Steps 8 to 10: a rule held by a test, so the next project with components cannot go without its picture. Skill
