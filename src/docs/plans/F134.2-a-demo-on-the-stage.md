# F134.2: A demo on the stage

> **Plan.** The second child of [F134](F134-the-showroom-looks-modern.md). The start became a stage in F134.1. This
> child brings a demo's own page onto the same stage, in the look of the K mockup, so walking from the start into a
> demo feels like the window you picked coming forward and growing.

**Status:** built unattended on Louis's word of 2026-11-15: "Finish the whole F134 unattended, stop and show before
merge. want to check locally". The look is the K mockup of F134's design, which he saw with the rest of it.
**Branch:** `feature/F134.1-the-start-is-a-stage`, where F134 is being finished.

## Context

Today a demo's page is a strip in its colour, a kicker, a heading and a lede, then the player with the cues in a
column beside it, the notes and the offer. It has its own look, from before the stage. The start now has a lit stage,
windows and a dock, so going from the start into a demo drops the visitor into an older page.

F134's design says what a demo's page becomes: "the same window grown to fill the stage, with what to watch as
chapters under it", the rail "as a slim dock of icons", and "a body for an agent at work". Its done-when: the five demo
pages play as they do today in the new look, at both widths, and the guide's stops are the same places.

**What was looked up.**

- *The stage today.* `DemoStage` is one component the five demo pages share. The three businesses reach it through
  the site's `BusinessDemo`, the coding agent and the triage team use it directly. Its places are sections with their
  own ids, `top`, `the-demo`, `what-to-watch`, `make-it-yours` and `ask`, and the guide's tour stops at them.
- *The window.* A business's player is `SiteMock`, which already draws a browser's bar with a live address over its
  website. So for a business the window is already there: it only needs the stage around it. The coding agent's and
  the triage team's players, `RecordedChat` and `TriagePlayer`, have no window around them.
- *The parts the start brought.* `LitStage` takes an accent and holds anything. `FramedWindow` takes an address and
  a body. `Dock` draws `DockItem`s in a row, with a divider where the group changes, and marks the current one.
- *The rail.* `DemoRail` draws each demo as a cover with its icon, its name and a line, grouped, beside the stage.
  `DemoChips` stands in for it on a phone.
- *The K mockup* (`F134-mockups/k-the-agent-runs-the-demo.html`): the head with a crumb of the demo's icon and its
  kind, the heading and the lede beside it; the window large in the light; the four cues as a glass row of numbered
  chapters on a progress line, with *Try it yourself* and *Play it again* at their end; the rail a slim glass column of
  icons at the side. Its agent bar is F134.3's, and its captions F134.4's.

## Design

**The stage is lit.** `DemoStage` puts everything it holds on a `LitStage` in the demo's accent, as the start does.
Its head follows K: a crumb with the demo's kicker, the heading as the page's one h1, and the lede beside it on a wide
screen. Its places keep their ids and their headings, so the guide's tour stops where it does today, and a test
holds the five ids.

**The player is the window.** A business's `SiteMock` is a window already, and stands in the light as it is. For the
coding agent and the triage team, the page puts the player in a `FramedWindow` with a word of where it runs: that is
the body for an agent at work. No new body is built, since `FramedWindow` holds any content.

**What to watch is chapters.** `DemoCues` draws the cues as a glass row of numbered chapters under the window, a line
of progress over them, the reached ones lit and the newest pulsing, each still a button that plays from its turn. On a
phone the chapters stack.

**The rail is a slim dock.** `Dock` gains `Upright`, a column instead of a row, and `DemoRail` becomes a `Dock` of the
site's demos, upright, each an icon with its name for a screen reader and as its title, the current one marked. The
covers' names and lines leave the rail, since the start shows them. `DemoChips` stays on a phone.

**What is reused.** `LitStage`, `FramedWindow` and `Dock` from F134.1, `DemoCues` and `DemoStage` as they are, no new
package.
