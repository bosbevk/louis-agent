# F166.4 Puls gone from louis-agent

A plan: Puls now lives in [its own repository](https://github.com/bosbevk/puls) (F166.3), so everything of it leaves
louis-agent, and what stays names Puls only as a product built elsewhere on the framework's packages. It is the fourth
and last child of [F166 Puls in its own repository](F166-puls-in-its-own-repo.md), and closes it.

## Context

F166's plan sets this child's done-when: no tracked file under `demos/puls`, the three packs gone, the full suite
green. What is Puls's here today, read from `main` on 2026-11-21: `demos/puls` (582 files), the Recipes, ShopPrices and
WhereToBuy packs with their checks, web views, tests and docs (about 390 files in `packs/`), 30 entries in the
solution, and a draft plan of Puls's own, `puls-track.md`. Outside those, 112 files still name Puls or its packs: 61
docs, 13 of the framework's tests (most of them rule tests whose lists name Puls's files), the framework's pages and
their content (6), the site's and the code-review app's Docker files and examples, two prompts, the rules Claude
reads, the README, the ROADMAP and the TODO. The commands F167 ran once in `src/tools/rename-projects` name Puls too;
they are the record of that rename and stay as they are.

Louis's picks, 2026-11-21, each of three options after research:

| # | The choice | His pick |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | How the framework's pages show Puls once it is gone | A: Puls stays, as the first product built on the framework from outside, linked to its repository under its new names; the Overview's `demos/puls` folder line goes |
| 2 | The three packs' ADRs (0069, 0078, 0082) | A: they move to `puls` beside 0081; louis-agent's index keeps their rows, pointing there, so no number is taken twice |
| 3 | How an example says where it lives | A: a small record, `ExampleHome`, a folder here or a path in a repository on GitHub, which answers what the page and its test ask (ADR 0123) |

One proposal for Louis's yes, with pick 1: ADR 0100 holds every claim on the site to the repo by a test. It gains one
narrow exception: a product built elsewhere is held by its link, a repository under `github.com/bosbevk`, since a test
here can't read another repository's files.

## Design

![Puls's folders and the three packs leave louis-agent, which names Puls only as a product built elsewhere](diagrams/F166.4-puls-gone-from-louis-agent-architecture.svg)

### 1. Puls, shown from outside

The *Examples* page keeps one list of examples. Each one's identity is an `ExampleHome`: either a folder in this
repository, as the coding agent's and the guide's are, or a path in a repository on GitHub, as Puls's is
(`bosbevk/puls`, `src/Puls.Identity`). The record answers its name, its link and whether it is here, so the page and
its test never work it out themselves. The test checks a home here on the disk, as today, and a home elsewhere by its
address. Puls's tiles name its parts as they are called now (`Puls.Packs.Recipes`). The Overview's folder list loses
`demos/puls`, and its line about the repo says Puls lives in a repository of its own, built on the packages.

![An example's identity is an ExampleHome, here or on GitHub](diagrams/F166.4-puls-gone-from-louis-agent-interface-design.svg)

![The Examples page's one list, Puls's home on GitHub and the others' here](diagrams/F166.4-puls-gone-from-louis-agent-objects-in-memory.svg)

### 2. The order

![The ADRs land in puls first, then the pages, the folders, what built them, and the docs](diagrams/F166.4-puls-gone-from-louis-agent-communication-the-order.svg)

The ADRs and the draft plan move to `puls` first, so nothing here ever points at a file that isn't there yet. Then the
pages change while the folders still exist, so their tests are seen failing for the right reason. Then the folders go,
with every rule test whose list names one of their files: those lists only shrink. Then what built or served Puls (the
Docker files that copy its docs, the examples in the code-review app's settings), the prompts that name its docs, and
last the docs, so each one says what is here today.

### 3. What is reused

The picks' patterns: `ExampleHome` as ADR 0123's value type. F166.3's `fix-links.pl` rule, by hand here, for a doc's link
that pointed into Puls: to the file in `puls` when it is there, else to louis-agent at the tag `puls-moved-out`. No new
package.

## Steps

Each step is one commit, with its test where it has code, and a stop after each for Louis's review. A push, a merge
or a tag waits for his word.

1. **The plan is kept** (louis-agent). This plan, its pictures, and its records.
2. **Puls's decisions go home** (`puls`). ADRs 0069, 0078 and 0082 beside 0081 in `docs/adr/`, and `puls-track.md` in
   `docs/plans/`, each with its links mended; Puls's docs link to them there. Its own pull request, merged before step 4.
3. **Puls, shown from outside** (louis-agent). Test first: `ExampleHome` answers its name, link and place; the
   *Examples* page links Puls to `bosbevk/puls` under its new names; the Overview names no `demos/puls`. Then the code,
   and ADR 0100's exception on Louis's yes.
4. **Puls's folders go** (louis-agent). `demos/puls` and the three packs' folders, their 30 solution entries, and every
   rule test's list entry that names one of their files. The build and the full suite green.
5. **What built or served Puls** (louis-agent). The site's and the code-review app's Docker files that copy Puls's
   docs, the settings examples that name its paths, `.gitignore` and the third-party notices, and the two summaries
   whose example is a Puls path.
6. **The prompts that name Puls's docs** (louis-agent). The guide's and the framework Orbi's `docs-map` skills, with
   their golden files regenerated and their diffs read.
7. **The docs say what is here** (louis-agent). The 61 docs, the packs' index, the rules Claude reads (the repo map,
   the design rules, two skills), the README, the ROADMAP and the TODO: Puls named as a product built elsewhere, a
   link into Puls mended, the ADR index's three rows pointing at `puls`. Every doc read against the repo as it is.
8. **The close** (louis-agent). The write-up, the records, and F166 closed with its four children: this plan and
   F166's folded and deleted.

## Verification

Step 3's tests, seen failing first. After step 4, the build and the full unit suite, then CI's integration tests, with
no project or path of Puls's left. Steps 5 to 7: the rule tests that read the repo, the golden files' diffs read, and
the docs' links checked (no relative link leading nowhere). The framework's site run on this PC and its *Examples*
and *Overview* pages looked at, at 1920 by 1080 and 390 wide. No paid run.

## Concepts, and where they sit in GOAL.md

- **A framework that names no product** (steps 4 to 7): the engine split finished, the only product here a demo of the
  framework's own. *Skill 20, extensibility.*
- **A claim held by its proof** (step 3): what the site says is held by a test, and where the proof lives elsewhere, by
  its link. *Skill 18, testing and shipping.*
