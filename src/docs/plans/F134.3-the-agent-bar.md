# F134.3: The agent bar

> **Plan.** The third child of [F134](F134-the-showroom-looks-modern.md). In the showroom the guide stops being a
> button in a corner and becomes a bar at the foot of the page: an orb that shows what it is doing, a box that is
> always there to type in, and its suggestions as chips. It is the same chat widget, with a second layout.

**Status:** built unattended on Louis's word of 2026-11-15: "Finish the whole F134 unattended, stop and show before
merge. want to check locally". The look is the agent bar of the J and K mockups, which he saw with F134's design.
**Branch:** `feature/F134.1-the-start-is-a-stage`, where F134 is being finished.

## Context

F134's rules for the guide, in Louis's words: "our agent button needs a proper redesign", "the agent must do it
all", "we should have like chatwidget layout type". Rule 3 of F134's design: the box is always there, and the guide's
choices are shortcuts, never the only way to answer. Rule 4: one engine with a layout, `Corner` as today or `Bar`, and
no second widget. Rule 5: the frame that draws the bar keeps the page clear of it.

F134's done-when for this child: the showroom's guide is the bar, a typed question is answered mid-walk, and no page
ends under the bar.

**What was looked up.**

- *The widget today.* `ChatWidget` is one component: a runner of its own (`ChatRunner`), a panel with the message
  list and the composer, a corner button, and the tour's button when folded. It hears a `GuideButton`'s question
  through the site's `GuideDoor`, and the guide's `ShowPage` calls through the runner's `ToolCalled`. Its code-behind
  is 367 lines with its comments, so what the bar adds goes in components of its own.
- *Where the showroom puts it.* The site's `ShowroomLayout` puts `ExpertChat`, the guide's widget, in the showroom
  frame's `Corner` slot. The frame keeps room at its bottom for the corner, `--corner-room`.
- *The mockups' bar* (`F134-mockups/agent.css`): fixed at the foot, centred, at most 760 wide. Chips above it. In it,
  the orb, a line of what the guide is doing over a box to type in, and a send button. The orb has four states: idle
  it breathes, thinking an arc goes round, showing rings go out to the page, speaking it pulses. The talk's cost in
  the bar and the captions are F134.4's and F134.5's.

## Design

**One widget, two layouts.** `ChatWidget` gains `Layout`, an enum `WidgetLayout` with `Corner`, the default and
today's look, and `Bar`. In `Bar` the widget draws `AgentBar` in place of the corner button and the panel. The
runner, the door, the tool calls, the pieces and the sending stay the widget's, so nothing is done twice.

**The bar.** `AgentBar` is the orb, a line of what the guide is doing, the composer, which is always there, and over
it the talk so far, folded to its last answer until the visitor opens it. Above the bar stand the suggestions as
chips while the guide is idle; a chip asks its question, and the chips always end with a word that the box is there
too.

**The orb is a general part.** `Orb` draws one of four states, `OrbState`: `Idle`, `Thinking`, `Showing` and
`Speaking`. `OrbStates.Of` is the rule, a plain class tested with no browser: streaming with no words yet is
thinking, words arriving is speaking, a place just shown is showing, and the rest is idle.

**The frame keeps clear.** A frame that draws the bar keeps its height free at the bottom, so no page ends under it.

**The showroom wears it.** The site's showroom layout gives its guide the bar; the rest of the site keeps the corner.

## Steps

One commit each, test first. Unattended.

1. **The plan**, its four pictures and its rows. *Test:* none (docs).
2. **A widget's layout.** `WidgetLayout`, and `ChatWidget.Layout`; `Bar` draws a bar with the composer showing and no
   corner button. *Test:* `Corner` draws as today; `Bar` draws the composer without being opened.
3. **The orb.** `Orb`, `OrbState` and the rule `OrbStates.Of`. *Test:* each state's class, and the rule's four
   cases.
4. **The bar.** `AgentBar`: the orb from the runner's state, the line of what it is doing, the talk folded to its
   last answer over the composer, and opened in full. *Test:* the bar's parts, the orb's state as the runner streams.
5. **The suggestions as chips.** `Suggestions` on the widget; chips while idle, each asking its question, ending with
   the word that the box is there. *Test:* a chip sends its words; none show while the guide works.
6. **The frame keeps clear.** The showroom's frame keeps the bar's height free. *Test:* the frame's rule.
7. **The showroom's guide is the bar.** *Test:* the showroom layout's guide is a bar with the site's suggestions; the
   rest of the site keeps the corner.
8. **Checked live, docs and done.** A walk of the start with a question typed mid-walk, by the real guide, its cost
   reported. The five pages at both widths, none ending under the bar. The web's and the site's design docs, the
   write-up, the work log, this plan deleted.

## Concepts

Skill 4 (tool design) and skill 17 (observability): the orb shows what the agent is doing, read from the runner's own
state, never guessed. Skill 20 (extensibility): one engine with a second layout, not a second widget.

## Approved during the build

- **2026-11-15, unattended.** Louis: "Finish the whole F134 unattended, stop and show before merge".
- **2026-11-15, picked while the plan was written, open for Louis to overrule.**
  - *The talk folds to its last answer.* The mockups give the talk to captions and a drawer, F134.4's. Until then the
    bar keeps the talk above it, folded to the last answer, and opens it in full on a press.
  - *The orb's rule reads the runner.* "Showing" lasts a short moment after the guide's `ShowPage`, since the runner
    says when a place was shown and not how long the visitor looks at it.
- **2026-11-15, step 2 as it was built.** `ChatWidget` gains `Layout`; in `Bar` it draws `AgentBar`, which holds the
  composer from the start, and no corner button or panel. The corner's markup is unchanged, its two goldens with it.
  The widget's code-behind went past the 200-line rule with the new parameter, so what a window's size means to its
  styles moved onto the size's own type, `ChatWindowSizes.ClassesOf` (ADR 0123): the guide's widget and a mock
  website's chat each wrote the same switch. The bar's styles come with its parts in step 4.
- **2026-11-15, step 3 as it was built.** `Orb` in `Components/Widget/Orb`, with `OrbState` and the rule `OrbStates.Of`,
  a switch on the three facts the chat gives: whether it is streaming, whether words have come, and whether a place
  was just shown. The look is the mockups', its colours the framework's variables, and it holds still under less
  motion. A screen reader hears who the orb is and its state.
