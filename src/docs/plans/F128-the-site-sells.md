## Context

Louis, 2026-11-09: "Our goal is to sell websites with agents, and custom agents, for now. The framework takes second
place to that." The review of every page (`Downloads/F128-findings.html`) found the offer seventh on the landing,
after six sections about the framework, a developer's hero, a menu led by the framework, no demo a business owner
recognises, a guide that invites developer questions, and several facts made wrong by FX122 and F124.

**Louis's picks, each of three:** 1A the hero sells the website with an agent; 2A the landing's order is offer
first, framework as proof; 3A two plans with what each includes (numbers not given: defaults Starter R500, 300
conversations and 30 minutes of changes a month; Business R1,000, 1,000 conversations and an hour; Louis to confirm);
4A recorded demos, "not a bakery, say a laywers practice, restaurant, and a doctors practice"; 5A the menu leads with
what we build, pricing, demos and contact, the framework last; 6A the guide asks what brings the visitor; 7A a "how
it works for you" band with the facts a buyer trusts.
