site's host (`LouisAgent.SiteFrameworkApi`), the triage team on the demo's host, and neither maps them.

## The children, in build order

1. **F126.5.1 The guide's evals.** A page given in the eval file. About ten conversations from the guide's rules,
   the prompt-injection case among them, then conversations from what the turn log shows. A live baseline of three
   repeats on Louis's go, and the replay test grown to every conversation's first repeat.
   *Done when:* the guide's eval holds a conversation per rule its voice and skills name and one per pattern the
   turn log showed, its baseline is a recorded live run, and `GuideEvalReplayTests` replays every conversation free.
2. **F126.5.2 The framework's Orbi.** The framework site's host maps the eval routes with a data folder and
   recordings, and Orbi gains an eval of its own (reading a doc, explaining code, walking a page), with its baseline.
   Its runs are read over the API, since the framework site has no admin pages.
   *Done when:* Orbi's eval runs live and replays free on the framework site's host.
3. **F126.5.3 The triage team.** The demo's host maps the eval routes, and the triage team gains an eval whose facts
   use `routed-to` and `continued` for the first time, with its baseline. F126 closes with it.
   *Done when:* the triage team's eval runs live and replays free, and F126's parent plan is folded and deleted.

Each child's plan is written when it starts, from what the child before it learnt.
