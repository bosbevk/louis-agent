Proposal notes · not on the real page

What changed from the Word CV, and why

Taken off (it's a public page)

  • ID number, street address, age, health, nationality: nobody needs them to hire you, and on a public page they're identity-theft material (POPIA).
  • Salaries at each job: they weaken your next negotiation.
  • The 1-to-5 skill ratings: recruiters don't trust self-ratings; grouped skills and proof read better.
  • School subjects, the course's module list, old tools (VB.NET, Java, PHP, SVN, Xamarin, Silverlight on its own).

Added

  • A one-line headline and summary pointed at AI engineering, with louis-agent as proof of work up top: in 2026, a link to working, measured code counts more than a list.
  • Every job rewritten as outcomes ("shipped OneFM's first release"), newest first.
  • Four facts with real numbers from the repo; a PDF version for recruiters' systems (same page, print styles).
