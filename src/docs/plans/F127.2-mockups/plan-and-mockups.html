Context

"the demos must be interactive, 3 pagers with working pages and sections, agents must demonstrate navigation between pages and sections. when the demo finishes there should be a button try it yourself." Louis, 2026-11-10

Today each mock website is one long page. Its agent's ShowPage scrolls that page to a section, and the conversation is a recording, so nothing runs while a visitor watches. The showroom is the site's selling point. A visitor who can click through a business's website, then talk to its agent themselves, sees what they would buy.

Done when

  • Each mock website has three pages a visitor can click through: the menu, a card that opens its own page, and back.
  • Each recording shows its agent opening a page, then a section on another page. The law practice's goes to Wills and estates.
  • When a recording ends, Try it yourself opens a live chat with that business's agent, in the same corner, through the open door under the shared cap. Its ShowPage moves the website as the recording's does.
  • A try-out booking reaches your mail with [demo] in its subject, and the agent tells the visitor it was a demo.
  • On a 390 px phone the chat leaves the website's upper part in view, and nothing in the mock draws over the chat.
  • The spotlight's ring never covers a neighbouring section, and every demo page has a way back to all the demos.
  • Each business wears its reworked brand: its mark, its faces and its palette, as mockup 0 draws them.
  • On a demo page the guide knows the demo and its state, helps the visitor try it, and, after the visitor says yes, sends a question to the live agent itself, whose answer shows in the demo.

Design

A mock website has pages

A site writes each page as <MockPage Path="/rooms" Label="Rooms">…</MockPage> inside the mock. Each page registers with its SiteMock through a cascading value, as Microsoft's TabSet example does, and the mock draws its menu from them. Every page is rendered and only the current one shows. That way the live chat's page reading sees every page's places, and a page change needs no re-render.

The shown address picks the page

The current page is the longest page path the shown address starts with: /practice/wills/after-divorce is on /practice/wills, /stay/breakfast on /stay, and / is home. That rule is a small pure class, MockPaths, tested on its own. A page change fades the old page out and the new one in, and stays still for less motion. The hero and the facts strip belong to home. The menu's words come from the pages, so NavItems goes.

A visitor can click

The menu items are buttons, and a card with its own page opens it. A page that isn't in the menu has a back link to its parent ("← What we do"). Clicks work while the recording plays and while trying live.

Try it yourself

The recorded chat's Ending slot (it exists) holds the button. Clicking it swaps in MockLiveChat: a live chat on the framework's ChatRunner, opened as the business, the website back home, with a hint of a first question. Watch the recording swaps back. Each turn sends the mock's page the way the guide's widget sends one:

  • the current page's address, its title and its visible text;
  • every page's places, by their addresses;
  • the menu, as links.

A ShowPage in the stream moves the mock exactly as a recorded one does. The live chat has its own store key, so a try never mixes with the guide's chats.

The door takes a list

OPEN_IDENTITY becomes OPEN_IDENTITIES (no alias, ADR 0049): the guide and the three businesses. A visitor's sessions per address per day stay one cap across them all. The door marks the identity it admitted, and the session is narrowed to that identity's own toolsets.

Demo leads

LeadsToolset gains demo-leads, and LeadsPack takes the toolset it is. The site registers it twice, and the showroom identities list demo-leads.

  • A demo lead's subject is [demo] Try-out lead: {Name}.
  • Its reply tells the model the request went to louis-agent, not to a real business, and to say so to the visitor. That's a tool text, not a prompt rule (ADR 0080).

The fixes found on 2026-11-10

  • The spotlight's ring starts at its section's own top line (inset: 0 -8px -8px), so it never reaches into the section above. The spacing stays as it is.
  • .mock-widget gets z-index: 3, above the banner's words (2) and the photo badge (1).
  • Below the mock's 760 px the chat is capped at about 40% of the page.
  • ← All demos sits in the showroom's bar, on every page but the start.

  1. The fixes from 2026-11-10. The ring starts at its section's own line, the chat above the mock, about 40% of the page on a narrow mock, and ← All demos on every page but the start. The guesthouse says beach: "A weekend by the beach, with the dog", in its heading, its line in the demos' list and its search description.   4. The door takes a list. OPEN_IDENTITIES, one cap across them, the session narrowed to the admitted identity, and the settings, compose files and Azure yaml. Tests: OpenDoorTests.
  5. Demo leads. demo-leads, the [demo] subject, and the demo replies. The site registers both packs, and the showroom identities use the demo one. Tests in the leads pack and the site, and the tool-text goldens regenerated.
  6. The live chat on the mock. MockLiveChat on ChatRunner, opened as its identity, sending the mock's page with each turn. Its ShowPage raises ToolCalled. Tests with the fixture API.
  7. Try it yourself. In BusinessDemo: the button at the play's end, the swap, the website home, the hint, and Watch the recording. Site tests, then one live try per business by hand.credit
  8. The voices and the recordings. Each voice learns pages, then places. Three conversations are recorded again, each opening a page and then a section on another page. The cues are re-pointed. A recording mails you only if you say so.credit

The stage marks its demo (the business, its identity, whether it's playing, ended or live), and the guide's page reading carries it with the live chat's last lines. AskTheDemo(text) in the site pack, carried out by the browser as ShowPage is, to be called only after the visitor said yes: the widget hands it to a DemoDoor, and BusinessDemo goes live and sends it. The guide's voice gets On a demo page. Tests for the tool, the call, the door, the swap and the page; one guided try per business by hand.credit
  11. Docs. ADR 0116 A showroom's demos can be tried live, through the open door, linked from ADR 0099. The project docs, .env.example, the films if their hash moved, the write-up, the work log and the TODO. The full suite runs, and the plan is deleted.

Concepts, and where they sit in GOAL.md

  • Tool design (skill 4): ShowPage stays a pure tool. The browser acts on the call, so one tool moves the real site and a mock of a customer's site. A demo lead's reply tells the model the truth.
  • Context engineering (skill 7): the page a live agent reads is built in code from what's on screen: the current page's text, every page's places, the menu.
  • Cost (skill 13): a live try spends real tokens. One cap per address across every open identity keeps the door's worst day what it is today.
  • Safety (skill 15): the door is an allow-list, closed by default. A visitor's session is narrowed in code to the identity it asked for and that identity's packs.
  • Extensibility (skill 20): one pack, two named instances. A site's pages compose by registering with their mock.
