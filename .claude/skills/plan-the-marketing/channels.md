# The nineteen channels

A table to look things up, for step 3 of [Plan the marketing](SKILL.md). The channels and their order are from
*Traction* by Gabriel Weinberg and Justin Mares, as its authors list them. The last column says what the channel
looks like for a small South African product sold to households, and is ours.

| # | Channel | What it is | For a product like ours |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Targeting blogs | Reaching the blogs and newsletters the customers read | A local news site, a consumer or money blog, a neighbourhood newsletter |
| 2 | Publicity | Coverage in newspapers, radio and news sites | A community paper first, then a national site, each with a story a reader can use |
| 3 | Unconventional PR | A stunt, or a kindness to customers that gets talked about | Checking a ward's bills for free at a hall on one evening |
| 4 | Search adverts | Paid adverts beside search results | Words people type when a bill jumps, in the three towns only |
| 5 | Social and display adverts | Paid adverts on social sites and banners | A Facebook card shown to home owners in one municipality |
| 6 | Offline adverts | Print, radio, billboards, flyers | A flyer at a library or a shop's notice board, a community paper's small advert |
| 7 | Search engine optimisation | Being found in search without paying | A page per municipality that answers "why is my water bill so high" |
| 8 | Content | Our own articles, guides and videos | The July tariff change explained each year, with worked examples |
| 9 | Email | Mail to people who agreed to it | A monthly note to users who asked for it (POPIA's section 69) |
| 10 | Viral | Customers bringing customers | A checked bill that is easy to share with a neighbour |
| 11 | Engineering as marketing | A free tool that draws the right people | The one free check a month, or a tariff calculator on the site |
| 12 | Business development | A partner who gains too | A ratepayers' association, an estate agency, a body corporate's managing agent |
| 13 | Sales | Selling one to one | Louis talking to the first users himself, by name |
| 14 | Affiliates | Paying for each sale someone brings | Someone who posts for a fee, marked `#ad` as the ARB asks |
| 15 | Existing platforms | The stores and the social networks themselves | The Play store's page and its search, a Facebook Page, a community group with its admin's yes |
| 16 | Trade shows | A stand where the buyers gather | A home show or a consumer fair, if one is in reach |
| 17 | Offline events | Our own or a sponsored gathering | A ward committee's or a ratepayers' meeting |
| 18 | Speaking | Talking to a room | Ten minutes at such a meeting, or on community radio |
| 19 | Community building | Bringing the customers together | A group where users compare what their municipality answered |
