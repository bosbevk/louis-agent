# Test jobs for check-a-product-idea

Three jobs to try this skill on, in a fresh session that did not write it (`/write-a-skill`, step 7). Each ask is
said the way Louis says it. The skill passes a job when the session loads it from the ask alone, everything in
*Must happen* happens, and nothing in *Must not* does.

| # | Where, and what Louis says | Must happen | Must not |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | No study exists. He says: "investigate the plausibility of a booking system for hair salons like Booksy" | The idea is said back as a hypothesis in three parts, with the AI-first version drawn before any rival is compared. Three or four rivals' own pricing pages are read that day, and every price is quoted as shown. The market's size is given with its publisher, or said to be not known. `origin/main` and the TODO are read, and each thing the product needs is held against what the framework has. The report ends with one verdict, what would change it, and a next step that costs no code. Every page that did not load is listed | A price, a market size or a count of businesses from memory. A design that is the rival's product with an agent added. A new TODO row, a changed build order, or a plan. Code |
| 2 | The CMMS study is in project memory. He says: "what about the cmms idea, did we talk to anyone yet? I spoke to two guesthouses, both said it sounds great" | The earlier study is read from memory and its report, not researched again. The two talks are held against the Mom Test: "sounds great" is a compliment, not a commitment, and that is said plainly. The verdict stays where it was, and the questions for the next three talks are given, about what they did the last time something broke | The verdict raised to "build it" on two compliments. The whole study run again. A pitch script that sells the idea to the buyer |
| 3 | He says: "I want to build 20 small AI apps on the framework, one for each trade, is that a good idea" | The idea is checked as one hypothesis: the problem, the solution and why us. What it rests on outside the repo is read at its source that day, the stores' rules on many similar apps from one developer first. What is not known is named. A verdict with what would change it, and a next step that costs no code | Twenty studies started. Odds or earnings given without a source. The answer given from memory because it "sounds like" an earlier thread |
