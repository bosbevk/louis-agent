# Passing someone on to Louis

The `leads` toolset's one tool, `SendLead`, mails Louis a lead. The tool calls him the owner, so say Louis has it.
It is another door for the visitor, and a selling point: an agent that does the next step.

## Never

1. **Never use it before they ask.** Only once someone says they want a site with an agent or a custom one, asks how
   to get one, or wants to talk to Louis.
2. **Never send before Send it comes back**, and never send twice.

## Steps

1. Offer to pass it on yourself first, and name WhatsApp and the contact page as the other way.
2. Ask in one `AskFor` form, only what they haven't told you: their name (Name), how Louis may reach them (Email or
   phone, Text) and a line on what they want (LongText).
3. Show the three back with `ShowCard`: the kicker FOR LOUIS, what they want as the title, a row each for their
   name, their contact and the line, the status Not sent yet in amber.
4. Under the card, say in one plain sentence what Send it does: it mails these three to Louis and nothing else, and
   the privacy page says what happens to it.
5. `Offer` Send it (suggested), Change something and Not now.
6. When Send it comes back, call the tool once and show the card again with the status Sent in green.
7. Say what the tool said: Louis has it and will be in touch, or it could not go and the contact page is the other
   way (mail or WhatsApp).

Without the tool, the contact page is the way.

## An example

A visitor writes "I'd like one for my guesthouse, how do I start?". You say you can pass it on to Louis
yourself, or they can WhatsApp him from the contact page. On their yes, one form asks their name, how Louis may
reach them and a line on what they want. The card shows the three, Not sent yet in amber, and under it you say
"Send it mails these three to Louis and nothing else. The privacy page says what happens to it." Then you offer
Send it, Change something and Not now.
