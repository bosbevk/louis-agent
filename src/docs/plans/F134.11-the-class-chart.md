# F134.11: The class chart, redone

> **Plan.** A child of [F134](F134-the-showroom-looks-modern.md): the `classes` picture, a project's interface
> design, drawn in the cards' look. A type is a glass card with a badge that says its kind, and a link's kind
> shows in its line: the structure in the accent, a use in grey.

**Status:** approved 2026-11-14 (Louis: "approve do unattended until done and then wait"). **Branch:**
`feature/F134.1-the-start-is-a-stage`, where F134.7 to F134.10 were built. **Design:**
[m-classes.html](F134-mockups/m-classes.html), opened in a browser from the repo.

## Context

Louis, 2026-11-14, with a screenshot of "louis-agent.diagrams: the interface design": "next will be F134.11
which will be the redesign of the interface diagram, first research then give me a design and wait". He was shown
one design, drawn from two real charts' data. Of its violet lines he asked "should these lines not also be
animated like the rest?", and of a flow's line with its word in a pill, "can we do it like this". Both are in the
design he approved.

**What was looked up.**

- *The code.* A class chart has no layout and no drawing of its own. `ClassFlow`, an Adapter, turns it into a
  flow: a type becomes a box with its kind as the box's note and its members under a divider, and a relation
  becomes an arrow whose dashes and word say its kind. `ClassesRenderer` then draws that flow with
  `PlainFlowDrawing`, the way every flow was drawn before F134.8, laid out by the older rules, where arrows
  share a trunk. Nothing else draws with `PlainFlowDrawing`.
- *The pictures.* 58 use this kind:

  | What | How many |
  |---|---|
  | Types in a chart | 3 to 21, median 9, 556 in all |
  | Types with no members | 144. The most members on one type is 13 |
  | Relations | 194 uses, 153 holds, 64 creates, 63 implements, 11 inherits |
  | Relations with a word of their own | 178 |
  | A type's kind, by its words | 76 interface, 75 record, 58 static, 21 enum, 4 abstract, 91 with none |
  | Types with no tone named | 457, coloured by kind: teal for a contract, green otherwise |
  | Types with an icon, charts with groups | none, and none |
  | Charts that run down | 2. The rest run right |
  | Charts over 1,600 wide | 25. The widest is 2,630 |

- *Why the screenshot reads badly.* Every link looks alike: a "holds" and a "uses" differ by dashes and a small
  word. And a name is never wrapped, so one long name makes its whole column wide.
- *The other kinds' looks.* F134.8 to F134.10 built `PictureLook` (dots that run along a line), `MarqueeLook`
  (the small title, the light, the glass) and, for a flow, the word in a pill on its line. `SvgWriter` draws one
  kind of arrowhead, at a line's end or start, in a tone.
- *The rules that will run.* `DocsDiagramsTests` holds each SVG to its JSON byte for byte and holds that every
  flow and class chart keeps its words and lines clear. `FolderSizeTests` allows six source files a folder.
- *The pattern.* The class chart stays an Adapter onto the flow's layout (Refactoring.Guru, *Adapter*). Stated
  from memory; no source was re-read for this plan.

**His picks, 2026-11-14.** Put as recommendations with the design, and approved with it:

- *Look only.* The boxes stand where the flow's layout puts them. A layout that packs a chart tighter is not in
  this child.
- *Arrows apart,* each at its own place, as a flow's have been since F134.8, and no shared trunk.
- *One design,* and no further two to pick from.
- *A line's colour says its kind,* and not the box it leaves: violet for structure, blue for "creates", grey for
  "uses".
- *What moves.* "Holds", "is a" and "creates" carry dots. "Implements" and "uses" stand still.

**What could not be confirmed.** How the design reads on a chart of 21 types, on one that runs down, and with
"is a" and "implements" links is not drawn yet. Step 5's golden and step 6's sheets show them.

## Design

**The picture, part by part.**

- *Its own title*, small in the top left corner, on a soft light, as a cards picture and a sequence have. The
  docs' frame gives a class chart only its note bar.
- *A type is a glass card.* A badge in the type's tone holds a letter for its kind: I an interface, R a record,
  E an enum, A abstract, S static, C anything else. A type that names an icon shows the icon instead. Its name
  stands beside the badge, bold, with its kind under it, and its members under a hairline, in the code font. A
  contract, an interface, a record or an enum, wears the accent along its top.
- *A long name wraps* at 30 characters, so one name no longer widens a column.
- *A link's line says its kind.*

  | Kind | Line | Its ends | Moves |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | holds | solid, violet | a diamond at the holder, an arrowhead at what it holds | yes |
  | is a (`inherits`) | solid, violet | a hollow triangle at the base type | yes |
  | implements | dashed, violet | a hollow triangle at the interface | no |
  | creates | dotted, blue | an arrowhead | yes |
  | uses | dashed, grey, thin | an open arrowhead | no |

- *A link's word* stands in a pill on its line, in the code font, edged in the line's colour: a flow's pill, by
  a flow's measures.
- *The key*, under a faint lit line: each tone the badges wear, each letter the picture has, and each kind of
  link it has, drawn as a short one of its own.
- *The colour.* The tones are on the badges only. The lines use the look's violet and blue and the theme's grey.

**The layout stays the flow's.** `ClassFlow` still turns a chart into a flow, and `GraphLayout` still places it,
now by the rules a flow uses, `FlowRules.Straight`. Two things change in what the adapter hands over: a long
name is wrapped, and every type asks for a badge's room beside its name, which a flow gives a box that has an
icon. A relation's kind is not in a flow's arrow, so the drawing reads it from the chart itself, relation by
relation in the order they were written.

**The writer learns three more ends.** A hollow triangle, a diamond and an open arrowhead, beside the arrowhead
it has. A page's copy keeps the ones a drawing uses.

**The old flow drawing goes.** After the switch nothing draws with `PlainFlowDrawing`, so it is deleted with
its tests.

**No ADR is added or changed.** ADR 0118, a diagram is data our renderer draws, holds as it is.

## Steps

One commit each, test first. Unattended, on Louis's word: no stop after a step, every pick recorded under
*Approved during the build*. A step that turns out too large to review in ten minutes is built as two commits,
and the plan says so.

1. **The plan**, its four pictures, the design in `F134-mockups/`, and its rows in the TODO, the work log and the
   plans' index. *Test:* none (docs).
2. **A line's ends.** `SvgWriter` can end a line in a hollow triangle or an open arrowhead and start it in a
   diamond, each written once, and a page's copy keeps the ones in use. *Test:* each end is written once and
   referenced by its line; an end no line uses is not written; a page's copy keeps them.
3. **The look.** `ClassesStyle`, names that start `cls-`: the badge by its tone, the name, the members, a line
   for each kind of link, the ends, the pill and the key. *Test:* a rule for every part; a line of each kind; it
   sets no variable of its own and repeats no name of a shared look.
4. **The badge's letter and the key.** `TypeMarks` says a type's letter from its kind's words. `ClassKeyLayout`
   says what the key holds and where. *Test:* each kind's letter, the first that fits; a picture with no "holds"
   has no such item; the items wrap when the picture is narrow.
5. **The drawing.** `ClassesDrawing` draws what the layout placed: the title, the light, the lines by their
   kind, the words in their pills, the cards and the key. The renderer still draws the old way, so no docs
   picture changes. *Test:* a golden with every kind of link and of type; a "holds" has its diamond and its
   dots and a "uses" has neither; a long name is drawn on its lines.
6. **The switch.** `ClassesRenderer` hands its picture to the new drawing, and the docs' frame gives a class
   chart only its note bar. `PlainFlowDrawing` goes. The 58 pictures are redrawn. *Test:* the docs' picture
   tests; no other kind of picture changed; every redrawn picture looked at on a sheet, light and dark.
7. **Documented and done.** *Draw a diagram* and its skill say how a class chart is drawn now. The diagrams'
   design doc, the write-up and the work log follow. F134's plan says this child is built, and this plan is
   deleted.

## Concepts

- Steps 5 and 6: an Adapter that keeps paying. The class chart never had a layout, so every fix the flow's
  layout got reached it, and the new look needs only a drawing. Skill 20 (extensibility).
- Step 2: a small general part added to a shared writer, a line's ends, where one kind needed it, in a form the
  next kind can use. Skill 20.
- Steps 5 and 6: the new way built beside the old and switched in one commit, with the old drawing deleted when
  its last user left. Skill 18 (testing and shipping).
