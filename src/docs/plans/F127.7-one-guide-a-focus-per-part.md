# F127.7 One guide, a focus per part

> **Plan**, F127's seventh child, after F127.6 (a site names its areas). Louis, 2026-11-12, asked what louis-agent.dev's
> chat portal is for: "it is for the whole website, it must preserve across the whole website, when on normal pages it
> talk about selling the product and tries to get people to buy the agent and goes through each section, as it is now.
> Then /demos it is a demo guide it focuses on guideing peopl through the demos, as it does now, then for the demos
> itself they exist in the showroom window and main guide talk to them via address. /framework our agent should be
> more technical that's why we have diagrams planned". His pick of three for how the guide switches: a focus skill,
> read when the focus changes. This file changes only under *Approved during the build*.

## Context

louis-agent.dev has one guide, kept across the site's layouts (FX135), with one voice and four skills: the page index
and the live demo always loaded, the site map and the docs map read on demand. Every message carries the page the
visitor reads. The voice holds the selling, the demo guiding and the technical answers together, so on a framework page
the guide still sells, and on a selling page it may go deep into code.

## Design

### What changes, in plain words

- **A site names its parts' focuses** through the builder (ADR 0120): `areas.Focuses(f => f.Default("selling")
  .Under("/demos", "demos").Under("/framework", "framework"))`. A page's focus is its longest matching address, else
  the default.
- **The guide's widget sends the focus** with the page on every message, and the API's page block says it in one line:
  `The part of the site: framework.`
- **The guide reads the focus's skill when the focus changes**: three on-demand skills, `focus-selling`,
  `focus-demos` and `focus-framework`, each holding that part's rules. Only the focus in use costs tokens, and the
  history stays append-only (ADR 0008).
- **The parts**: selling on the ordinary pages (walk each section, work toward a site with an agent, as now); the demos
  on /demos and each demo's page (guide through the demos; a business answers in its window, asked by address); the
  framework on /framework (technical: code, docs named, and diagrams once F138 gives them).

## Steps

Each step test first, one commit, stopping for Louis's review.

1. **The plan**, its four pictures drawn.
2. **A site names its focuses**: `Focuses(…)` on the builder, `SiteFocuses.Of(path)` (the longest matching address,
   else the default; a site that names none sends none), and the guide's widget fills the page's `Focus`. Tests:
   `SiteFocusesTests`, the widget's page tests.
3. **The page block says the focus**: `PageRequest.Focus` on the wire, and the API's page block (the whole page and the
   in-view line alike) says the part of the site. Tests: the API's page-block tests; the wire's round trip.
4. **The guide's three focus skills**, on demand, and one voice rule: read the part's skill when it changes, before
   answering. The selling rules that only hold on the ordinary pages move from the voice into `focus-selling`; the
   framework skill asks for docs named and code when asked, and says diagrams come with F138. Tests: every focus has a
   skill with a header; the voice's rule.
5. **louis-agent.dev names its focuses**: selling by default; demos on /demos and every demo's address (from
   `SiteDemos`); framework under /framework. Tests: every page of the site gets a focus that has a skill.
6. **Checked live and documented**: the site on the PC, one guide carried from the landing to /demos to a framework
   page, each part answering in its focus, at desktop and 390 px; the docs (the guide's and the web's design docs, ADR
   0114 amended, the work log, the write-up); the plan deleted.

## Verification

## Approved during the build

- **2026-11-12, after step 1: a piece the visitor selects** (Louis, on the landing's three cards: "each part on the
  page becomes a piece the agent can elaborate on, and focus on. like select it"; his picks of three: click it, a chip
  in the chat, the guide explaining it at once; and into F127.7). The focus narrows from the part of the site to one
  piece on the page. Every piece already has its address (ADR 0107), so the framework's web makes a site's pieces
  selectable: a click on a piece (not on a link inside it) rings it, and the guide's box shows a chip, *About: It
  answers while you sleep ×*. The guide explains the piece at once, and the messages after it carry the piece as their
  focus until another is picked or the chip is closed. Two steps join the plan, after step 3:
  - **3b. A piece the visitor selects** (framework web): the pieces selectable, the ring, the chip on the widget's box,
    the explanation asked on the click, and the selected piece's address and heading sent with the page
    (`PageRequest.Selected`), said in the page block. Tests: the click selects and asks, a link inside still follows,
    the chip closes the selection, the block says the piece.
  - **4b. The guide's rule for a selected piece**: explain that piece, from the page's words and its docs, and stay on
    it while it is selected. Tests: the voice's rule.
- **2026-11-12, step 2** (Claude, unattended): the widget filling the page's focus moves to step 3, with
  `PageRequest.Focus` it needs. The focuses are an area like the others (`Focuses(…)` adds one named `focuses`), in
  `Components/Areas/Focuses/`; a part matches its address and every page under it by whole segments, the longest part
  winning, and a page's query, anchor and host are set aside.
- **2026-11-12, step 3** (Claude, unattended): the API's prompt left `TurnStream` for `Sessions/Prompts/`

- **2026-11-12, after step 3b: the guide selects, from anywhere** (Louis, on the landing's number tiles and the
  identities' deck: "we can get rid of these cards, they can become colored", "the coloured cards get a pressed state,
  the agent must be able to select each one going through the tour", "from anywhre that matter", "they should also get
  Page Urls all of them inorder for the agent to go from one page to another and selct it"). A step **3c** joins:
  - every piece has its own address in the page's order, as a card has (ADR 0107): `/home/it-answers-while-you-sleep`
    (the id drops its `piece-` prefix to read like a card's), listed to the guide with the page's places;
