# CL65.2: The picture builder

> **Plan.** The second child of [CL65](CL65-the-diagrams-as-an-engine.md). Six kinds put a picture together by
> the same steps, each in its own method. One builder does it now, and each kind directs it. No picture changes
> by a byte.

**Status:** approved 2026-11-14 with its parent (Louis: "approve go unattended"). **Branch:**
`feature/F134.1-the-start-is-a-stage`.

## Context

Louis, 2026-11-14, of what builds a diagram: "then use builder pattern for implementation." His pick of three
shapes: one fluent picture builder, with each kind's renderer its director.

**What was looked up.** Each kind's drawing was read line by line, since the builder must write the same bytes.

- *The same in all six:* the light's fade is defined first. The shapes come in one order: the light, the title,
  the type, the body, the key, the caption. A caption takes 34 and stands 8 above the bottom. The shared look's
  rules come first in the style and the kind's own last.
- *What differs, and must stay different.*

  | Kind | Room above | Body to key | The light | Width at least the title's | Its own fades, in order |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | flow | 44 | 26 | behind the top, 30 down, 190 deep | yes | edge |
  | class chart | 44 | 26 | the same | yes | edge, glass |
  | composite | 44 | 26 | the same | yes, and its type's | edge |
  | sequence | 48 | 12 | behind the top, 36 down, 170 deep | yes | beam, back, life, edge, glass |
  | cards | 38 | no key | one behind each highlighted card | no | rail, badge, edge, glass |
  | components | its layout's | its layout's | behind the top, 30 down, 190 deep | no | none |

- *The body.* Five kinds draw it as one group, moved down by the room above. The component diagram draws
  straight into the picture, at places its layout already moved down.
- *The key.* Three kinds draw it as a group, two straight into the picture. It is laid out for the picture's
  final width, which is only known once the title and the type are measured.
- *The keys' rows.* Four classes place a key's items by the same loop with the same five numbers: a heading of
  34, a row of 28, 10 between a sample and its words, 6.6 a character, 30 between two items.
- *The renderers.* Five of seven only hand on to their drawing. `Svg/Renderers` holds six files, its limit, so
  the composite's renderer stands beside its drawing already.
- *The ticket* is a card in a chat with a look of its own. It keeps its own renderer and is not built this way.

## Design

**`PictureBuilder`.** Fluent, one a picture, in the engine's `Svg/Renderers/Parts`.

- `PictureBuilder.Titled(title, measures)` starts one. `PictureMeasures` holds the four numbers that differ: the
  room above, the room between body and key, and how far down and how deep the light is. Its default is the
  flow's.
- `.Type(type)`: what type of diagram it is, at the right of the title's line. None for a kind that has none yet.
- `.Paint(id, run, stops)`: one of the kind's own fades, after the light's, in the order called.
- `.Lights(draw)`: the kind's own lights, where it has no light behind its top.
- `.Body(cls, drawn, width, height)`: the body as a group. `.Draw(draw, width, height)`: a body drawn straight
  in. `.Narrow()`: the picture is as wide as its body, and a longer title does not widen it.
- `.Key(layout)`: a function from the picture's final width to the key's height and how to draw it.
- `.Caption(words)` and `.Style(rules…)`.
- `.Build()` works out the width and the height, writes the parts in their one order, and returns the SVG.

**A kind's drawing is the director.** It places with its own layout, draws its body and its key with its own
code, and hands them to the builder. The kinds stay a Strategy: `DiagramRenderers` still picks one renderer by
kind.

**One placer for a key's rows.** `KeyRows.Place` holds the loop and the five numbers. Each kind's key layout
says what its items are and how wide each sample is, and no more.

**A renderer stands beside its drawing.** Each kind's renderer moves into its drawing's folder. A flow's three
files and the component diagram's three get a folder each, so `Parts` holds only what every kind shares.

## Steps

One commit each, test first. Unattended. After every step the docs' tool must write no picture and no golden
may change.

1. **The plan**, its four pictures and its row in the plans' index. *Test:* none (docs).
2. **The builder.** `PictureBuilder` and `PictureMeasures`, used by nothing yet. *Test:* the parts come in their
   one order; the width is the body's or the title's and type's; the height adds the room above, the body, the
   key and the caption; the fades are defined in the order called, after the light's; a narrow picture is not
   widened; a kind's own lights replace the one behind the top.
3. **The six kinds are built through it.** `PictureParts.Open` and the three copies of the light go. *Test:* the
   goldens and every docs picture, unchanged; no drawing writes a title, a light or a caption of its own.
4. **One placer for a key's rows.** *Test:* its rows wrap at the width and start at the left; the four layouts
   place as before, by their own tests.
5. **A renderer beside its drawing.** Nine files move. *Test:* the solution builds, every test passes.
6. **Documented and done.** The design doc, the write-up, the work log, the parent's plan, this plan deleted.

    `Parts/Flows`, the component diagram's three to `Parts/Nesting`, and the cards', the sequence's and the
    class chart's renderers beside their drawings. `Svg/Renderers` itself holds the ticket's renderer alone.
  - *Their tests moved to mirror them,* with the flow's golden beside its test.
  - *No picture changed.*
- **2026-11-14, step 2: the builder.**
  - *`Heading(words)` was added to the design:* a component diagram writes its title and what stands under
    it on the title's line, and is still named by its title alone. So the words in the line and the
    picture's name are two things.
  - *`PictureMeasures.Usual` is a flow's, a class chart's and a composite's,* and a sequence hands in its own.
  - *Nothing is written before `Build`,* since the key is laid out for the final width.
  - *Used by nothing yet,* so no picture can change in this step.
- **2026-11-14, step 3: the six kinds are built through it.**
  - *Each drawing's `Of` is a few lines now:* it places, and tells the builder its fades, its body, its key,
    its caption and its styles. A sequence and a cards picture hand in their own measures. A cards picture
    hands in its own lights, and it and the component diagram are narrow.

    title's room and holds the key. Its heading is its title and what stands under it.
  - *`PictureParts` keeps the panel and a tone's class.* Its opener, the light and the title's measures are
    the builder's. The opener's tests went with it: the builder's tests and the goldens hold the same.
  - *A rule test holds it,* in `DiagramsProjectTests`: no file under the engine's renderers but the builder
    writes a title, a caption or the SVG itself, and none but a cards picture a light. It was written after
    the drawings were moved, so it was run against the drawings as they were to see it fail.
  - *All 268 pictures and every golden are the same, byte for byte.*
- **2026-11-14, step 4: one placer for a key's rows.**
  - *`KeyRows.Place` takes the item's type and how to read and move one,* so it knows no kind. It is in a new
    `Keys` folder of the engine, since three of its four users are layouts and one a drawing.
  - *Each layout keeps its own `Heading`, `Row` and `Words`,* now equal to the placer's, since their drawings
    and tests read them by those names.
  - *No picture changed.*
