# Test jobs for open-a-pull-request

Three jobs to try this skill on, in a fresh session that did not write it (`/write-a-skill`, step 7). Each ask is
said the way Louis says it. The skill passes a job when the session loads it from the ask alone, everything in
*Must happen* happens, and nothing in *Must not* does.

| # | Where, and what Louis says | Must happen | Must not |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A cloud thread, on a fix's branch with `main` merged in and the suite green. He says: "PR" | The title is `fix(fxnn): what it gives`. The body opens with the thread's two "Requested by Louis" lines, then what the fix means in one or two sentences, then the sections by their names in bold. The body is read back once it is open. Louis is the assignee | A "Generated with Claude Code" or "Generated by Claude Code" footer, or a session link. A `##` heading in the body. A section that says "n/a" |
| 2 | A branch that rewrote four guides, with no id. He says: "open the PR" | The title is `docs(guides): …`. *Tests* names the Docs tests that ran, and *Not checked* names what was not looked at | A title that starts `Docs:`. *What was wrong*, since it is not a fix. A claim that something was checked when it was not |
| 3 | A pull request is open, and a commit was pushed after his review. He says: "update the PR" | The same pull request's body gets the commit's line under *What changed* and under *Tests* | A second pull request. The body rewritten from nothing, losing what was there |
