Chakalaka

Ingredients:

  • Oil for frying
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2 chillies, finely chopped
  • 2 teaspoons garlic, minced
  • 1 teaspoon ginger, grated
  • 2 tablespoons mild curry powder
  • 1/2 green, red and yellow pepper, diced
  • 4 carrots, grated
  • 1 tomato, grated
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • 1 can baked beans
  • Salt and Pepper to taste
