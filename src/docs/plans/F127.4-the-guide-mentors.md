## Context

**What Louis asked:** F127.3 left the guided live demo to this plan (its picks 7, L2: "the guide asks on the server,
the page shows it in the business's window, and the visitor carries on in the same session"; 8, C2: "the window shows
the talk, the card shows the work"). On 2026-11-12, choosing what the guide does when a visitor must decide, he
added: "when the guide must give options to the user, like you do for me", picked the option cards' look from the
design page ([the canvas](https://claude.ai/artifact/6U2hR349TuHdTz9pa2xEV7), the cards on the guesthouse's board),
said of the booking summary "this becomes a readonly card", and "the agents must draw this themselves with our
diagram tool".

**What exists:** `AskAgent` asks a business on this instance and streams its events into the guide's delegation card
(F127.3.1); the guide's widget hides its work (`ShowWork="false"`), so no tool call is drawn there; a tool call the
page acts on is read from the stream by its name (`ShowPageCall`, an Observer); a business's live window
(`MockLiveChat`) opens its own session through the open door; the diagrams library draws flows, cards, sequences and
classes (CL60), and the web already references it for `GraphView`.
