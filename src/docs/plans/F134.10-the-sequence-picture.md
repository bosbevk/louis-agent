# F134.10: The sequence picture, redone

> **Plan.** A child of [F134](F134-the-showroom-looks-modern.md): the `sequence` picture, who calls whom in order,
> drawn in the cards' look. A participant is a badge and a name with no box, a lifeline is a faint lit rail, and a
> call is a beam that runs from violet to blue.

**Status:** approved 2026-11-14 (Louis: "go"). **Branch:** `feature/F134.1-the-start-is-a-stage`,
where F134.7, F134.8 and F134.9 were built. **Design:** [m-sequence.html](F134-mockups/m-sequence.html), opened in
a browser from the repo.

## Context

Louis, 2026-11-14, with a screenshot of "louis-agent.diagrams: drawing one picture": "now 134.10 now we need to
redo this one. same as with the others, research, so design, plan create". Three designs were drawn from that
picture's real data. He asked how the key would look on the third, saw it drawn, and picked it: "C and start".

**What was looked up.**

- *The code.* `SequenceLayout.Place` stands the heads in a row, each lifeline as far from the others as the words
  between them need, a longest path from the left. `SequenceRows` walks the steps into rows and frames each block
  round its own. `SequenceRenderer` draws the frames, the dashed lifelines, each arrow in its sender's tone with
  its words in a chip above it, the notes and the heads. Every measure, a head's size, a line's height, a block's
  tab, is a constant in one class, `SequenceText`, which the layout, the rows, the renderer and a placed row all
  read.
- *The pictures.* 64 use this kind:

  | What | How many |
  |---|---|
  | Participants in a picture | 2 to 8, most 4 to 6 |
  | Calls, and answers | 405, and 237 |
  | Notes | 17, of which 4 stand over several lifelines |
  | Blocks | 40: 18 `alt`, 17 `loop`, 5 `opt`, nested up to three deep |
  | Calls a participant makes to itself | 31 |
  | Participants that are a person | 16 |
  | Pictures that number their steps | 3 |
  | Steps in a picture | 5 to 27 |
  | The longest message | 105 characters |

- *The tones.* Every one of the 320 participants has a tone, and 304 have an icon. Today a line is drawn in its
  sender's tone.
- *The cards' look.* F134.9 built it: `CardsStyle` holds the light, the glass with its accent edge, the pill and
  the small title, with names that start `crd-`, and `SvgWriter` can define a paint. A sequence in the same look
  wears several of those same parts.
- *The rules that will run.* `DocsDiagramsTests` holds each of the 64 SVGs to its JSON, byte for byte, and holds
  that every sequence keeps its words inside the drawing, by the layout's own measures. `FolderSizeTests` allows
  six source files a folder: `Svg/Renderers/Parts` holds six, so the new drawing gets a folder of its own, and
  `Svg/Theme/Looks` holds four.
- *The pattern.* Fowler's *Introduce Parameter Object*: a group of values that travel together becomes one value
  that is handed in. Stated from memory; no source was re-read for this plan.

**His pick before the plan, of three options (2026-11-14).** *How design C's measures come in while the pictures
still draw the old way:* "Hand the layout its measures". The numbers move out of the constants into one value with
two instances, today's and design C's. One layout serves both until the switch, and then today's is deleted.

**What could not be confirmed.** How design C reads with eight participants, with blocks three deep and with a
call a participant makes to itself is not drawn yet. Step 2 draws them, and the plan stops there until Louis has
seen them.

## Design

**The picture, part by part.**

- *Its own title*, small in the top left corner, as a cards picture has. The docs' frame gives a sequence only
  its note bar.
- *A participant is a badge and a name.* Its icon stands in a badge filled with its tone, and its name under the
  badge, with no box. A person with no icon wears a person's icon, as today. A soft light stands behind the row
  of badges.
- *A lifeline is a faint lit rail*, from a node under its participant's name down the picture, fading out.
- *A call is a beam.* A line that runs from violet at its sender to blue at its receiver, with a glow, a node
  where it leaves and an arrowhead where it arrives. Dots run along it. Its words stand above it, bold.
- *An answer is quiet.* A thin dashed grey line with an arrowhead, its words above it in the quiet colour. It
  stands still.
- *A call a participant makes to itself* leaves its lifeline, turns and comes back, with its words beside the
  turn.
- *A note is a glass panel* over the lifelines it names.
- *A block is a glass frame* with the accent along its top. Its kind stands in a pill, with its label beside it,
  and each other case of an `alt` under a dashed divider with `else` in a quiet pill. A block inside another sits
  inside its frame.
- *A numbered picture* puts each step's number in a small ring where its line starts.
- *The key*, in one row under a faint lit line: an item for each tone the picture's badges wear, a call, an
  answer, a note when the picture has one, and each kind of block it has. `alt` reads "one case or the other",
  `loop` "repeats" and `opt` "only if".
- *The colour.* One accent on every call, the palette's violet running to its blue. The tones are on the badges
  only, so a call no longer has its sender's colour: the cost Louis was shown with the design.
- *The words fit by a generous measure*, as a flow's and a card's do, since they are set in the sans font.

**The measures are handed in.** `SequenceText`'s constants and its functions become one record,
`SequenceMeasures`. It has an instance for today's picture and, from step 4, one for design C. `SequenceLayout`,
`SequenceRows` and the renderer take the one they are handed. A placed row carries where its arrow runs, which it
worked out from a constant until now. The step that does this changes no picture, and the proof is that the
docs' tool writes none. At the switch today's instance is deleted, and the record stays with one instance: the
measures of a sequence, as a value.

**What the cards and the sequence share moves to one place.** Both wear the small title, the light, the glass
with its accent edge, the pill with its code and a node. Those rules move from `CardsStyle` to a shared
`MarqueeLook`, with names that start `mrq-`, as F134.8 moved what a flow and a component diagram share into
`PictureLook`. `CardsStyle` keeps what is a card's own, and `SequenceStyle` adds a sequence's own. The 45 cards
pictures then change in their class names only, and the proof is that they are the same images, shot before and
after.

**No ADR is added or changed.** ADR 0118, a diagram is data our renderer draws, holds as it is.

## Steps

One commit each, test first, a stop after each. A step that turns out too large to review in ten minutes is built
as two commits, and the plan says so.

1. **The plan**, its four pictures, the picked design in `F134-mockups/`, and its rows in the TODO, the work log
   and the plans' index. *Test:* none (docs).
2. **The design, for what the first mockup leaves open.** Design C drawn for: a call a participant makes to
   itself, blocks three deep, a `loop` and an `opt`, a note over several lifelines, numbered steps, a person, eight
   participants, a message over several lines, and the light look. Louis sees them before step 3. *Test:* none
   (docs).
3. **The layout takes its measures.** `SequenceText` becomes `SequenceMeasures`, with one instance, today's. The
   layout, the rows, the renderer and the docs' check read the one they are handed. A refactor: nothing a reader
   sees changes. *Test:* the sequence's 26 tests and its golden pass unchanged, and the docs' tool writes no
   picture.
4. **Design C's measures.** `SequenceMeasures.Marquee`: a head as a badge and a name, words above their line, a
   block's tab as a pill. *Test:* with numbers, laid out by the new measures: no two heads overlap, every message's
   words fit between its lifelines, a block frames its rows, and a call to oneself keeps its words clear of the
   next lifeline. Over the 64 real pictures, every word stays inside the drawing.
5. **The looks.** What the cards and the sequence share moves to `MarqueeLook`, and `SequenceStyle` adds a
   sequence's own: the badge by its tone, the name, the beam both ways, the answer, the lifeline, the ring of a
   number. *Test:* each rule is written in one place; both schemes set every variable a rule reads; the 45 cards
   pictures change in their names only and are the same images, shot before and after.
6. **The key.** `SequenceKeyLayout` says what the key holds and where: the tones the badges wear, a call, an
   answer, a note and each kind of block, only where the picture has one. *Test:* a picture with no note and no
   block has neither item; the items stand in one row and wrap to a second when the picture is narrow.
7. **The drawing.** `SequenceDrawing` draws what the layout placed by design C's measures: the title, the light,
   the lifelines, the blocks, the calls and answers, the notes, the heads and the key. The renderer still draws
   the old way, so no docs picture changes. *Test:* a golden with every kind of step; a call has its beam, its
   node and its dots, and an answer has none of the three; a call to the left runs its beam the other way.
8. **The switch.** `SequenceRenderer` hands its picture to the new drawing with design C's measures, and the
   docs' frame gives a sequence only its note bar. Today's measures, the old renderer's tests and its golden go.
   The 64 pictures are redrawn. *Test:* the docs' picture tests; no other kind of picture changed; every redrawn
   picture looked at on a sheet, light and dark.
9. **Documented and done.** *Draw a diagram* and its skill say how a sequence is drawn now. The diagrams' design
   doc, the write-up and the work log follow. F134's plan says this child is built, and this plan is deleted.

## Verification

- Each step runs `louis-agent.diagrams.tests` and `louis-agent.doc-diagrams.tests`, and `louis-agent.core.tests`
  when it adds a folder or a file. Step 8 also runs `louis-agent.web.tests`, for the chat's drawn card.
- Each picture is looked at as an image, light and dark, before its step is committed.
- The full suite of unit tests runs before the pull request, with `main` merged in.
- No paid run.

## Concepts

- Step 3: a refactor proved by what does not change. The measures move from constants to a value, and not one
  byte of a picture moves with them. Skill 18 (testing and shipping).
- Steps 3 and 4: one layout serving two looks by the numbers it is handed, where F134.9 needed a second layout
  because the idea itself was new. Skill 20 (extensibility).
- Step 5: one shared look under two kinds of picture, moved to one place when the second kind arrives and not
  before. Skill 20.
- Steps 3 to 8: the new way built beside the old and switched in one commit. Skill 18.
