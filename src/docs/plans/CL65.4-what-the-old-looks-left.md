# CL65.4: What the old looks left

> **Plan.** A child of [CL65](CL65-the-diagrams-as-an-engine.md), built after CL65.6 and before CL65.5. Every kind
> of picture was redone in one look, a kind at a time, and each step left a little of the old behind. This child
> takes it out, so the engine holds only what a picture is drawn with today.

**Status:** approved 2026-11-14 with its parent (Louis: "approve go unattended"), and built unattended on his
"finished unattended" of the same day, read as "finish it unattended".
**Branch:** `feature/F134.1-the-start-is-a-stage`.

## Context

The list is the one [F134's plan](F134-the-showroom-looks-modern.md) kept as each kind was redone, under *Left for
the project refactor*, with what CL65.1, CL65.3 and CL65.6 added to it in the parent's plan. Its parent's
done-when for this child: the list is empty, and the docs' tool writes no picture.

**What was looked up, item by item.**

- *The old rules in the shared style.* `SvgStyle.Of` is in every picture's file. Read against every picture of
  the docs and every golden, 19 of its rules are for classes no drawing writes: `box`, `label`, `note`, `edge`,
  `dashed`, `group`, `group-label`, `word`, `word-chip`, `icon`, `frame`, `card`, `highlight`, `title`, `chip`,
  `code`, `text`, `badge` and `badge-icon`, and with them what a tone adds for a box, a label, an edge, a word
  and a badge. Some of those names still stand in the old mock-ups under `plans/F134-mockups`, which are files
  of their own with their own style and are not drawn by the tool. What is still written: `ground`, `arrow`
  and its tones, and `caption`.
- *The old rules in the frame's style.* `SvgStyle.Frame` is added to every framed picture. Seven of its rules
  are for what the frame no longer draws, a title bar and a legend: `titlebar`, `title-icon`, `title-rule`,
  `heading`, `title-head`, `legend` and a `swatch` a tone. The bar's colours are a palette's `Banner`, which
  nothing else reads since FX156.
- *The two layout rules nothing draws by.* `FlowRules.Joined`, which is `JoinedArrows` and `CentredRows`. Every
  drawing and the web's `GraphView` hand the layout `FlowRules.Straight`. `Joined` is still what
  `GraphLayout.Place` falls back to, so 47 calls in the tests lay a flow out by rules no picture is drawn by.
  Measured, with the fallback switched for a moment: 8 of the 714 tests fail, and the other 706 hold under
  the rules that are drawn.
- *The class that gives a box its column.* `Ranks` is 302 lines. It gives each box its rank, and it also leads
  a long line through the columns it passes: `Through`, `Straightened`, `Joined` and what they call.
- *The kinds' styles in two folders.* `Svg/Theme/Looks` holds the two shared looks and four kinds' styles, and
  is full at six files. `Looks/Kinds` holds two more. The ticket's is in `Svg/Theme`.
- *A word's pill, twice.* `FlowStyle` and `ClassesStyle` each have the same two rules, for the pill a word
  stands in and for the word. And three keys each have the same rule for the faint line over them: `flow-rule`,
  `cls-rule` and `seq-rule`.
- *The diagrams' own interface picture* names five renderers of seven, and not `PictureBuilder`.
- *From CL65.1:* one test project tests both projects, and one design doc covers both.
- *From CL65.3:* a cards picture whose file has no `"links"` is not refused. `CardsSpec.Links` is then nothing,
  and the reader's check of the links fails on it with .NET's own error.
- *From CL65.6:* a path is written two ways, an arrow of the flow's router with room round every letter and
  every other path with none. An arrow's numbers are kept to one decimal. The look's glass and its accent are
  defined with the same stops in five drawings.
- *Two names for Louis.* The `components` kind reads as UML's component diagram and shows what UML calls a
  composite structure. The `ticket` kind is drawn as a glass card since FX156.

**What was already done** by an earlier child, and is off the list: the three keys' placing and the composite's
(CL65.2, `KeyRows`), the light each kind drew itself (CL65.2), and the renderers' folder (CL65.2).

**What could not be confirmed** before the build:

- Whether the two interfaces behind `FlowRules` are asked by anything but the rule that stays. Step 3 reads the
  router and says.
- Whether an arrow's numbers can be kept exact without a picture changing. Step 8 tries it and counts.

## Design

**Only what is drawn stays.** A rule of a style, a layout rule or a colour that no picture uses is deleted, with
its tests. Nothing is kept for a kind that might come back (ADR 0055, ADR 0049).

**A strategy with one strategy is a class.** When `JoinedArrows` and `CentredRows` go, `IArrowRule` and
`IRowRule` each have one implementation, and `FlowRules` one value. ADR 0055 says an interface with one
implementation is a class, so the layout asks `ApartArrows` and `LevelRows` themselves, and the two interfaces
and the record go. Picked unattended, open for Louis to overrule: the seam can stay if he wants a second rule
tried again.

**What two kinds share is in the shared look.** The pill a word stands in and the faint line over a key move to
`PictureLook`, with names that start `pic-`, as a panel and a tone already are.

**A picture that changes is proved, not looked over.** Three steps rewrite pictures: step 6 by a class's name
and two rules of the style, step 7 by the style's text, step 8 by spaces in a path. Each is held against the
commit before by a script that takes exactly that change out again, as CL65.3 and CL65.6 did, and what is left
must be the same to the byte.

**The two names are put, not picked.** A kind's name is in every picture's JSON. Neither is renamed here.
Each is written up as a finding with three options and a recommendation, in the parent's plan, for Louis.

## Steps

One commit each, test first where behaviour changes. Unattended.

1. **The plan**, its four pictures and its row in the plans' index. *Test:* none (docs).
2. **A cards picture with no links is refused by name.** *Test:* the reader refuses it with a message that names
   `links`, seen failing on .NET's own error first.
3. **The two layout rules nothing draws by go,** and the strategy with them. *Test:* the 8 tests that rest on
   them are read one by one: one about a rule that goes is deleted with it, one about the layout is made to hold
   under the rules that are drawn. The docs' tool writes no picture.
4. **The long lines out of `Ranks`,** a pure move. *Test:* the docs' tool writes no picture, and the moved lines
   are the same to the byte.
5. **The kinds' styles in one folder.** *Test:* the docs' tool writes no picture.
6. **One rule for a word's pill and one for a key's line.** *Test:* a rule test, that no kind's style has a
   rule the shared look has. Flows, class charts and sequences are rewritten, by a class's name and the style
   only, proved by taking those out.
7. **The old rules out of the shared style and the frame's,** and the banner out of the palette. *Test:* every
   class a rule of the shared style names is one a drawing writes. Every picture is rewritten, by its style's
   text only, proved by taking the deleted rules out of the picture before.
8. **One way to write a path.** An arrow is written as every other path is, and an arrow's numbers are tried
   exact. *Test:* the path writer has one way. The pictures with arrows change by spaces in a path only, proved
   by reading each path as its letters and numbers.
9. **The look's fades in one place:** a fade is a small type, and the look names its own three. *Test:* no
   drawing writes a fade's stops. The docs' tool writes no picture.
10. **Documented and done.** The interface picture drawn from the code, the design doc, the two names put to
    Louis in the parent's plan, the write-up, the work log, F134's list emptied, this plan deleted.

## Concepts

- Step 2: a refusal that names what a person wrote, where a library's own error names nothing. Skill 5

  Skill 20 (extensibility).
- Steps 6 to 8: a change to every file proved by taking the change out again. Skill 18 (testing and shipping).
