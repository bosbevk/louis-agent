# F127.4.3 A form the agent draws

> **Plan**, a third child of F127.4 (the guide mentors), after F127.4.1's cards. Louis, 2026-11-12, on Seemeeu waiting
> for his name: "can the agent offer a card where I can put my name in?", "and then only that card". His picks, each of
> three: both places (a business's window and the guide's corner); a new `AskFor` tool. This file changes only under
> *Approved during the build*.

## Context

A business's agent and the guide ask for a person's details one thing at a time, in words: "What name should I put
the request under?", then a contact, then a line. Louis typed his name into the guide's chat instead of the business's
window, and answers in words get lost or mixed. The site pack already draws read-only cards (`ShowCard`) and choices
(`Offer`); an `Offer` ends the turn (FX138), and the guide ends each answer with choices (FX139) except while a
business waits for the visitor (FX141).

## Design

### What changes, in plain words

- **An agent can ask with a form:** `AskFor(question, fields)`, one to four fields, each a label and a kind (a name, a
  phone number, an email address, or text). It ends the turn, as `Offer` does.
- **The chat draws it as the only card:** one input per field and Send. Send answers as the visitor, in the fields'
  words ("Name: John · Phone or email: 066 391 2470"), and the card folds to what was sent.
- **A turn-ending tool closes an answer:** an answer that ends with a form owes the guide no choices (FX139 counted
  only `Offer`).
- **The agents ask with it:** Seemeeu, Wheatfield and Koringland when they need a guest's details, and the guide when
  it takes a lead, in one form instead of three questions.

## Steps

Each step test first, one commit.

1. **The plan**, its four pictures drawn.
2. **`AskFor` in the site pack:** the tool, its field record and kind, its check (one to four fields, a label of up to
   30 characters each, no label twice, a question), its texts, `[EndsTurn]`. Tests: `AskForTests`.
3. **A turn-ending tool closes an answer** (`TurnRunner`): an answer that called any tool marked `[EndsTurn]` owes no
   closing tool. Tests: `EndsEachAnswerTests`.
4. **The form card in the web:** `AskForCall` reads the call, `FormCard` draws an input per field (its kind's input
   type), Send waits until each field is filled and sends them in their words, and the card folds to what was sent.
   Tests: bUnit, with a golden.
5. **Only that card:** `BlocksView` draws the form under the words, and a turn that holds a form draws no other cards.
   Tests: `BlocksView`'s.
6. **The agents ask with it:** the three businesses' voices and the guide's lead flow. Tests: their prompts. Then a
   local run in Chrome, in Seemeeu's window and the guide's corner, at desktop and phone width; the goldens; the docs
   (ADR 0117's F127.4 section, the site pack's design doc, the work log, the write-up); the plan deleted.

## Verification

- Each step: the tests of what it touches; `core.tests` when names or folders are added.
- Step 6: the site run locally, a booking in Seemeeu's window and a lead in the guide's corner through the form, with
  `RESEND_API_KEY=not-a-real-key` so no mail goes out; the guide's live calls on Louis's test credit, the cost reported.

## Concepts, and where they sit in GOAL.md

- **Tools that shape the conversation** (the agent tools skill): a tool that asks the person in a fixed shape and ends
  the turn, so the model waits and the answer comes back whole.

## Approved during the build

- **2026-11-12, both kinds of input, and a rule against type cards** (Louis's pick of three, on the guide offering a
  "Describe the job" card that only said to type). `FieldKind` gains `LongText`, a box that grows, beside the one-line
  `Text`: `{ Name, Phone, Email, Text, LongText }` (step 2 checks it, step 4 draws it as a `textarea`). Step 6 adds one
  guide rule: whenever it asks the visitor to write something (their job, their details), it asks with `AskFor`, never
  a choice that only says to type; the custom-agent flow asks for the job with one `LongText` field.
